# US-07 — Browse vault in Obsidian

**As a user**, I can open my Obsidian vault at any time and navigate my wiki as ordinary markdown files so that I can read and explore my knowledge base independently of the autowiki interface.

## Acceptance Criteria

- All pages use standard Obsidian `[[wikilink]]` syntax.
- Pages are organized in human-readable folders with prose content, not machine-generated structures.
